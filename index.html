Vanishing Stars: The Impact of Light Pollution

For millennia, humans looked up and saw a sky awash with stars. Today, artificial lighting has created a permanent "skyglow" over our neighborhoods, hiding the cosmos behind a veil of orange and gray.

Light pollution doesn't just steal our view of the Milky Way; it disrupts nocturnal wildlife ecosystems, wastes massive amounts of energy, and affects human circadian rhythms.

What is your sky quality?

Astronomers use a specific metric to measure how badly a night sky is affected by artificial light. It's called the Bortle Scale. Click on the menu above to learn how the scale works and to find the nearest dark sky sanctuary near your neighborhood.
